A practical product research checklist
Field What to record Why it matters
Product link The specific listing URL and selected variant. A category link is a browsing entry point, not a confirmed item.
Price and currency Displayed item price, quantity, currency, and date checked. Prices and exchange rates can change; delivery may cost extra.
Size and measurements The listing’s size chart, units, and relevant dimensions. Fit varies across products and measurement methods.
Materials and photos Stated composition, construction details, and available views. Appearance alone cannot confirm composition or authenticity.
Seller and terms Seller identity, availability, shipping conditions, and return rules. The actual transaction depends on the destination’s current terms.

Fit

Measure something you own.

For clothing, compare a comfortable garment with the listing’s dimensions using the same measurement method. Ask about missing measurements.

Evidence

Keep unknowns visible.

If a material, seller claim, or product detail cannot be verified, mark it as unknown rather than filling the gap with a guess.

Budget

Compare delivered cost.

Use the full estimated cost to compare options. Check whether shipping, service charges, taxes, or duties are included.

Wheebuy sizing guide & FAQs

Clear answers.
Useful next steps.

Understand what this guide provides, where its links lead, and which details still need checking.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Wheebuy spreadsheet means an organized product research list for people searching that phrase. Useful entries include a product link, category, listed price, available sizes, and notes to verify. This page is a browsing guide, not an official Wheebuy spreadsheet or a downloadable catalog.

Where do the links on this page go?

All navigation and shopping links on this page lead to CAFANS. Category buttons open the corresponding CAFANS category. This page does not process purchases.

Are the photos exact products available through these links?

No. The photos are editorial illustrations of fashion categories. They do not establish availability, authenticity, seller identity, or an exact match to a linked listing.

How should I compare product prices?

Compare the current item price, currency, quantity, shipping, service charges, and any applicable taxes or duties. A product price alone is not the total delivered cost.

What should I check before choosing a size?

Check the individual listing’s measurement chart, units, fit notes, and measurement method. Compare clothing measurements with a garment you own and footwear measurements with the listing’s specific sizing guidance.

Does this guide verify branded items?

No. References to Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès are for brand identification and style research. Verify seller information, provenance, and product details independently; names and photographs alone do not prove authenticity.
